CORE PRINCIPLE

[CONCEPT]
Risk management is the process of identifying, analyzing, and controlling potential financial losses.

[INSIGHT]
The goal is not to avoid losses but to ensure long-term survivability and prevent catastrophic drawdowns.

[RULE]
If capital preservation is ensured → Then long-term profitability becomes achievable

STOP-LOSS STRATEGY

[CONCEPT]
A stop-loss is a predefined price level where a position is exited to limit losses.

[INSIGHT]
It represents the point where the trade thesis is invalidated.

[RULE]
If price reaches stop-loss level → Then exit position immediately

[CONCEPT]
Fixed-percentage stop-loss ignores market volatility.

[INSIGHT]
This can lead to premature exits or excessive risk.

ATR-BASED STOP LOSS

[CONCEPT]
Average True Range (ATR) measures market volatility.

[FORMULA]
ATR Stop Loss (Long) = Entry Price − (ATR × Multiplier)

[INSIGHT]
Adjusts stop-loss based on volatility to avoid premature exits.

[FORMULA]
ATR Stop Loss (Short) = Entry Price + (ATR × Multiplier)

[INSIGHT]
Provides dynamic risk buffer for short positions.

[TABLE_ROW]
Market_Condition: High Volatility
ATR_Multiplier: 1.5–2.0
Strategy: Tight risk control

[TABLE_ROW]
Market_Condition: Moderate Volatility
ATR_Multiplier: 2.0–2.5
Strategy: Balanced approach

[TABLE_ROW]
Market_Condition: Low Volatility
ATR_Multiplier: 2.5–3.5
Strategy: Wider stop-loss

[RULE]
If volatility increases → Then widen stop-loss using ATR

[RULE]
If volatility decreases → Then tighten stop-loss

TRAILING STOP

[CONCEPT]
Trailing stop adjusts dynamically as price moves in favorable direction.

[INSIGHT]
Locks in profits while allowing trend continuation.

[RULE]
If price moves in favorable direction → Then adjust stop-loss upward

[RULE]
If price reverses → Then stop-loss remains fixed

POSITION SIZING

[CONCEPT]
Position sizing determines how much capital is allocated per trade.

[FORMULA]
Position Size = (Account Equity × Risk %) / (Entry Price − Stop Loss)

[INSIGHT]
Ensures consistent risk per trade.

[RULE]
If risk per trade exceeds 2% → Then reduce position size

[RULE]
If account balance decreases → Then reduce position size proportionally

FIXED FRACTIONAL MODEL

[CONCEPT]
A fixed percentage of capital is risked per trade (typically 1–2%).

[INSIGHT]
Automatically reduces risk during drawdowns and increases during growth.

KELLY CRITERION

[CONCEPT]
Kelly Criterion determines optimal capital allocation for maximum growth.

[FORMULA]
f = (bp − q) / b

[INSIGHT]
Maximizes long-term growth but introduces high volatility.

[RULE]
If using Kelly Criterion → Then use fractional Kelly (25%–50%) to reduce risk

VOLATILITY-BASED SIZING

[FORMULA]
Position Size = Risk Amount / (ATR × Multiplier)

[INSIGHT]
Maintains consistent risk across assets with different volatility levels.

[RULE]
If asset volatility increases → Then reduce position size

RISK-REWARD RATIO

[CONCEPT]
Risk-reward ratio compares potential loss to potential gain.

[FORMULA]
Risk-Reward Ratio = Potential Loss / Potential Gain

[INSIGHT]
Determines trade quality and profitability potential.

[RULE]
If Risk-Reward ≥ 1:2 → Then trade is acceptable

[RULE]
If Risk-Reward < 1:1 → Then avoid trade

EXPECTANCY

[CONCEPT]
Expectancy measures average profit per trade.

[FORMULA]
Expectancy = (Win Rate × Avg Win) − (Loss Rate × Avg Loss)

[INSIGHT]
Determines long-term profitability of a strategy.

[RULE]
If Expectancy > 0 → Then strategy is profitable

[RULE]
If Expectancy < 0 → Then strategy leads to losses

BREAK-EVEN WIN RATE

[FORMULA]
Break-even Win Rate = 1 / (1 + Risk-Reward Ratio)

[INSIGHT]
Shows minimum win rate required to avoid losses.

MARKET FRICTIONS

[CONCEPT]
Market frictions include commissions, slippage, and transaction costs.

[INSIGHT]
These reduce actual profitability compared to theoretical returns.

[RULE]
If trading frequency is high → Then impact of transaction cost increases

DIVERSIFICATION

[CONCEPT]
Diversification reduces risk by spreading investments across assets.

[INSIGHT]
Reduces unsystematic risk but not systematic risk.

[CONCEPT]
Unsystematic risk is asset-specific and can be diversified.

[CONCEPT]
Systematic risk affects the entire market and cannot be eliminated.

[RULE]
If portfolio is diversified → Then unsystematic risk decreases

HEDGING

[CONCEPT]
Hedging involves taking offsetting positions to reduce risk.

[CONCEPT]

[CONCEPT]
